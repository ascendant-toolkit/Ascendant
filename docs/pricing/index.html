Plans, billing and refunds

What is the difference between Basic and Full?

Basic is for learning: the Learn, Watch, Explore and Real world sections of every chapter. Full adds the exam side: Practise, Exam questions, Tips and keywords, unit assessments and topic tests. Every chapter is built the same way, so you always know where things are.

How does the marking work?

Multiple choice and number questions are marked instantly, so you know straight away whether you were right. Longer written answers are self-marked against the mark scheme. Comparing your answer with how the marks are awarded is one of the best ways to learn what examiners reward and how to move up a grade.

Will more be added?

Yes. We keep expanding the content, the subjects and the features, and your plan covers what is added to it. Homework tracking is planned and is not available yet.

What does priority support mean?

Messages from Full and All-Access members are answered before the others. Write to support@ascendant.academy or use the contact form on the website.

Can I cancel any time?

Yes. Cancel whenever you like and you keep access until the end of the period you have paid for. Monthly plans stop at the next month, annual plans run to the end of the year, and there are no further charges.

Do you offer refunds?

There is a 14-day money-back guarantee on your first purchase. If it is not for you, get in touch within two weeks and we will refund it in full, no questions asked.

What if I change my mind part-way through an annual plan?

After the first 14 days an annual plan is non-refundable and you keep full access until it ends. If you have a genuine reason, get in touch: any goodwill refund is worked out at the standard monthly price for the months you have used, not the discounted annual rate.

Which currency am I charged in?

Prices are shown in US dollars. Prices include VAT and sales tax, so there is nothing added at checkout. You are billed in your local currency and the tax is handled for you.

Why is annual cheaper?

Paying for the year up front saves you around 17% against paying monthly: roughly two months free.
