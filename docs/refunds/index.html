Refund policy

How refunds and cancellations work.

Last updated 15 November 2026

14-day money-back guarantee

There is a 14-day money-back guarantee on your first purchase. If it is not for you, get in touch within two weeks of buying and we will refund it in full, no questions asked.

Monthly plans

Cancel at any time and you are not charged again. You keep access until the end of the month you have paid for. After the first 14 days, monthly payments are not refunded.

Annual plans

After the first 14 days an annual plan is non-refundable and you keep full access until it ends. If you have a genuine reason, get in touch: any goodwill refund is worked out at the standard monthly price for the months you have used, not the discounted annual rate.

Schools and tutors

Licences are annual commitments with no mid-term refunds. Schools get a 30-day evaluation instead. After that the licence runs for the agreed term.

How to ask

Use the contact form and choose Billing or refunds, or reply to your Paddle receipt. Approved refunds go back to your original payment method and your access ends when the refund is made.

Your legal rights

This policy does not affect any rights you have under consumer law.
