Privacy notice

What we collect, why, and who handles it.

Last updated 15 November 2026

What we collect

Your name, email address, the subjects you study, your progress and assessment attempts, and any message you send through the contact form. If you buy a plan, Paddle collects your payment details and billing address; we receive your email, the plan and the payment status, but never your card number.

Why we use it

To run your account, unlock the lessons you have paid for, show your progress, reply to your messages and send account emails such as password resets. We do not sell your data and we do not use it for advertising.

Who handles it

Supabase hosts our database and logins. Paddle processes payments as merchant of record. Resend delivers our account emails. GitHub hosts the website. Each handles data only to provide its service.

Cookies and storage

We use your browser's local storage to keep you signed in and remember your progress. We do not use advertising or cross-site tracking cookies.

How long we keep it

While your account exists. Payment records are kept as long as tax and accounting rules require. Ask us to delete your account and we will remove your data, except records we are legally required to keep.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete the personal data we hold about you, or to object to how we use it. Use the contact form and choose the topic that fits. Students in a school may also be able to ask their teacher to correct details.

Children

Many of our students are under 18. Younger students should use the site with a parent or guardian's agreement, and any purchase should be made by an adult.
