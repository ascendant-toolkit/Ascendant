Questions

Who is it for?

Cambridge IGCSE and A-Level students in Economics and Computer Science, the teachers who teach them, and schools and tutors who want one place for lessons, practice and progress.

Which exam board is this for?

Cambridge International only: IGCSE Economics 0455, IGCSE Computer Science 0478, A-Level Economics 9708 and A-Level Computer Science 9618.

Can I try it first?

Yes. A free account opens four full sample lessons, so you can judge the quality before paying anything.

Is it a replacement for a tutor?

For most students it covers what a tutor does week to week: explaining the topic, setting practice, marking it and showing what to fix. Many students use it alongside their class teacher, and some use it instead of paid tutoring.

Can my school or tutoring business use it?

Yes. Schools and tutors get a licence for a set number of students, free teacher accounts, and dashboards to see each student's progress. See the plans page or get in touch.

Does it work on a phone?

Yes. Every page works on phones, tablets and laptops, in light or dark mode.

Is this official Cambridge material?

No. This is an independent resource and is not endorsed by or affiliated with Cambridge University Press & Assessment. Cambridge IGCSE and Cambridge International AS & A Level are trademarks of their owners.
