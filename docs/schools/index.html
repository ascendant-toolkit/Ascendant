Questions

What does a seat cover?

One student in one subject for a year, with every lesson, interactive activity, marked assessment and progress report for that subject.

Is there a minimum order?

Licences start at 25 students per subject.

Can different departments buy separately?

Yes. Each subject can be bought and invoiced on its own, so a department can decide for itself.

What if a student changes subject?

Your teachers can move a student between the subjects on your licence from their dashboard, within the seats you have bought for each.

What happens at renewal or if we stop?

Licences are annual commitments with no mid-term refunds. If a licence ends, students keep their progress and lose access to the lessons.

Do you offer a refund?

Schools get a 30-day evaluation instead. After that the licence runs for the agreed term.
