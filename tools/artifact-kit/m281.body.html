Definition of the Market Economic System

What a market economy is, its key features and how it answers the basic economic questions. Syllabus 2.8.1.

The big idea

A market economic system is an economy where resources are mainly privately owned and the price mechanism, through the decisions of consumers and firms, answers the basic economic questions of what, how and for whom to produce. Firms are motivated by profit, consumers by satisfaction, and the government has a limited role.

Key features

FeatureIn a market economy
OwnershipPrivate individuals and firms own most resources and businesses
Decision-makingMade by millions of consumers and producers, not by the government
PricesSet by demand and supply: they signal, give incentives and ration
MotiveFirms seek profit; workers seek wages; consumers seek satisfaction
CompetitionFirms compete for customers, which keeps prices low and quality high
Consumer sovereigntyConsumers' spending decides what is produced
GovernmentLimited: law and order, property rights, defence

How it answers the three questions

  • What to produce: whatever consumers will pay for at a profit.
  • How to produce: the cheapest efficient method, as firms compete on cost.
  • For whom to produce: those willing and able to pay.

No country has a pure market economy: even the most market-oriented economies have some government provision, regulation and taxation, so they are better described as mixed.

Q1 Define 2 marks Define a market economic system.
ReminderResources allocated by the price mechanism.
A market economic system is an economy in which resources are allocated by the price mechanism with mainly private ownership and little government intervention.
Q2 Identify 2 marks Identify two features of a market economic system.
ReminderPrivate ownership, price mechanism, profit motive, competition.
Private ownership of resources. Prices set by demand and supply (or competition between firms).
Q3 Explain 4 marks Explain how a market economic system answers the question 'what to produce'.
ReminderDemand, price, profit, resource movement.
Consumers' spending shows what they want. When demand for a good rises, its price and profit rise, so firms produce more of it and resources move into that market.
Q4 Explain 4 marks Explain what is meant by consumer sovereignty.
ReminderConsumers decide what is produced by spending.
Consumer sovereignty means consumers decide what is produced. Firms produce what consumers spend on, because that is profitable, and stop producing goods that consumers will not buy.

Top tips

  • Ownership, prices, profit, competition. The four features to remember.
  • Link to what, how, for whom. Use the price mechanism.
  • No pure market economy exists. Most are mixed.
  • Say 'consumer sovereignty'. A key term.
