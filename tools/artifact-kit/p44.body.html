Shifts of a PPC

Causes and consequences of outward and inward shifts of the production possibility curve and what they show about growth. Syllabus 1.4.4.

The big idea

A shift of the PPC changes the economy's productive capacity. An outward shift means economic growth: more of both goods can be produced. An inward shift means capacity falls. Shifts are caused by changes in the quantity or quality of factors of production, including technology.

Outward shifts (economic growth)

CauseExample
More resources (quantity)Immigration, discovery of oil, new factories (investment), more people starting businesses
Better resources (quality)Education and training, better healthcare, technology, new methods
Supply-side policiesInvestment in infrastructure, training programmes, reducing red tape

Inward shifts

  • War, natural disasters (floods, earthquakes), depletion of resources, emigration or brain drain, population decline.

Types of shift

  • Parallel outward shift: capacity for both goods rises (general improvement in technology or labour).
  • One-sided shift: capacity rises for only one good, for example new farming technology: the curve moves out on one axis only.

Consequences

  • Economic growth: higher potential output, raising incomes and living standards over time.
  • Consumers can enjoy more of both goods; the opportunity cost of any given output falls.
  • Unemployed resources are not used up: growth (shift) is different from using idle resources (a move to the PPC).
  • An inward shift lowers output and living standards.

Capital goods today make growth tomorrow: producing more of them now can shift the PPC outwards in the future.

Examples are described in general terms so they stay accurate. Check current details before quoting figures.

The Green Revolution

New seeds and farming methods in the mid-20th century raised food output in many countries, shifting their PPC outwards.

Exam use: Technology as a cause of growth.

Oil discoveries

New oil and gas finds add to a country's resources and capacity.

Exam use: More land (natural resources).

Natural disasters

Earthquakes and floods can destroy factories and infrastructure and reduce capacity for some years.

Exam use: An inward shift.

Education in South Korea

Heavy investment in education raised productivity and supported fast growth.

Exam use: Quality of labour.

Q1 Explain 4 marks Explain two causes of an outward shift of a PPC.
ReminderTechnology, education, immigration, investment.
New technology could raise productivity, so more of both goods can be produced with the same resources. Immigration increases the labour force, so the economy has more resources and can produce more.
Q2 Explain 4 marks Explain what an inward shift of a PPC might show. (Feb/Mar 2024, P22 style)
ReminderLoss of resources; war, disaster, depletion; lower capacity.
An inward shift shows a fall in the economy's productive capacity. It could be caused by war or a natural disaster that destroys factories, or by workers emigrating. The economy can now produce less of both goods.
Q3 Analyse 6 marks Analyse how economic growth can be shown on a PPC. (Oct/Nov 2021, P22 style)
ReminderOutward shift; causes; effect on output and opportunity cost.
Knowledge (K)Application (A)Analysis (N)Evaluation (E)
Economic growth is shown by an outward shift of the PPC. It could be caused by more or better-quality resources, such as new technology or training. The economy can then produce more of both goods, so living standards could rise. Growth in capacity only raises output if resources are fully used and demand is sufficient.
Q4 Analyse 6 marks Analyse how advances in technology could affect a country's PPC.
ReminderProductivity; outward shift; possibly one-sided; growth.
Knowledge (K)Application (A)Analysis (N)Evaluation (E)
New technology can raise productivity and output per worker. This shifts the PPC outwards, so more goods can be produced with the same resources. If the technology benefits only one good, such as farming, the curve may shift out on one axis only. The country can then enjoy more of both goods in future. The gains depend on how quickly the technology spreads and on the cost of developing it.

Top tips

  • A shift needs a change in resources. A move to the PPC does not.
  • Name the cause and the factor. Quantity or quality of land, labour, capital, enterprise.
  • Outward shift = economic growth. Say it.
  • Draw both curves and label PPC₁ and PPC₂. Show the direction.
  • Evaluate: do the resources get used? Capacity is not the same as output.
