Calculation and Interpretation of PES

Calculating PES, interpreting its value and drawing supply curves for different elasticities. Syllabus 2.7.2.

The big idea

PES is calculated with the formula PES = % change in quantity supplied ÷ % change in price. Because supply slopes upwards, PES is positive. The value tells us whether supply is perfectly inelastic, inelastic, unitary, elastic or perfectly elastic.

Calculating PES

% change = (new − old) ÷ old × 100
PES = % change in quantity supplied ÷ % change in price

Example: price rises from $20 to $25 (+25%) and quantity supplied rises from 200 to 230 (+15%). PES = 15 ÷ 25 = 0.6: inelastic.

Interpreting the value

PESNameMeaningCurve
0Perfectly inelasticQuantity supplied does not change at allVertical
Between 0 and 1InelasticQuantity changes by a smaller % than priceSteep; meets the quantity axis if extended
1UnitaryQuantity changes by the same % as priceStraight line from the origin
Greater than 1ElasticQuantity changes by a bigger % than priceFlatter; meets the price axis if extended
InfinityPerfectly elasticProducers will supply any quantity at one price; any lower price means noneHorizontal

Any straight-line supply curve through the origin has PES = 1 at every point, whatever its slope.

Q1 Calculate 3 marks The price of a good rises from $20 to $25. Quantity supplied rises from 200 to 230. Calculate PES. (May/June 2023, P22 style)
Reminder%ΔQs ÷ %ΔP.
%ΔP = 5 ÷ 20 × 100 = 25%; %ΔQs = 30 ÷ 200 × 100 = 15%. PES = 15 ÷ 25 = 0.6 (inelastic).
Q2 Explain 4 marks Explain what a PES of 2.5 means.
ReminderElastic; quantity responds more than price.
A PES of 2.5 means supply is elastic. A 10% rise in price would cause quantity supplied to rise by 25%. Producers can respond strongly to price changes.
Q3 Identify 2 marks Identify the PES value for a perfectly inelastic supply curve.
ReminderZero.
Zero (0). Quantity supplied does not change.

Top tips

  • PES = %ΔQs ÷ %ΔP. Always use percentages.
  • Show every step. Percentage changes first.
  • PES is positive. Supply slopes up.
  • Through the origin: PES = 1. Whatever the slope.
  • State elastic or inelastic. Finish the answer.
