Arguments For and Against the Market Economic System

Advantages and disadvantages of the market economic system and how to evaluate them. Syllabus 2.8.2.

The big idea

The market economic system has advantages, mainly efficiency, choice and innovation, and disadvantages, mainly inequality, market failure and instability. To evaluate it, weigh both and conclude. The strength of each depends on the good (a luxury or a necessity) and on the people affected.

Advantages

  • Efficiency: competition pushes firms to cut costs and use resources well
  • Consumer choice and sovereignty: firms produce what consumers want
  • Innovation: profit rewards new products and methods
  • Flexible response: prices adjust quickly to changes in demand and supply
  • Incentives to work and take risks: rewards for effort and enterprise
  • Low administration costs: no large planning bureaucracy

Disadvantages

  • Inequality: goods go to those who can pay, so low-income households go without
  • Under-provision of public and merit goods (street lighting, education)
  • Over-provision of demerit goods and external costs such as pollution
  • Monopoly power: a dominant firm may raise prices and restrict output
  • Instability: booms and recessions, unemployment
  • Neglect of unprofitable goods and some regions

Evaluating the market system

  • It works well for goods where competition is strong and there are few external effects.
  • It performs poorly for goods with spillovers or where consumers lack information.
  • This is why most countries mix market forces with government intervention (see 2.10).

A good evaluation says what the system does well, what it does badly, and a judgement depending on circumstances.

Q1 Explain 4 marks Explain two advantages of a market economic system.
ReminderEfficiency, choice, innovation.
Competition between firms encourages efficiency so costs and prices are kept low. Consumers have a wide choice of goods because firms produce what consumers demand in order to earn profit.
Q2 Explain 4 marks Explain two disadvantages of a market economic system.
ReminderInequality, under-provision of public goods, externalities, monopoly.
Goods go to those who can afford them, so low-income households may go without essentials. The market ignores external costs such as pollution, leading to over-production of some goods.
Q3 Discuss 8 marks Discuss whether a market economic system is the best way to allocate a country's resources.
ReminderEfficiency, choice, innovation vs inequality, externalities, public goods. Judgement.
Knowledge (K)Application (A)Analysis (N)Evaluation (E)
In a market system the price mechanism allocates resources. Competition encourages efficiency and innovation, and consumers get what they want. There is little government cost and workers and firms are rewarded for effort. However, the market gives goods to those who can pay, so inequality can be large. It ignores external costs and under-provides public goods such as defence. Monopoly power can also raise prices. Overall, the market is good at efficiency but poor on fairness and the environment, so most countries need government intervention as well.

Top tips

  • Weigh both sides. Then give a judgement.
  • Use pairs: efficiency vs equity. Clear contrasts.
  • Link to market failure. Externalities, public goods, monopoly.
  • Use 'depends on' evaluation. The type of good matters.
  • Most countries are mixed. A final judgement.
