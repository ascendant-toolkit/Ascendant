Points Under, On and Beyond a PPC

What it means when an economy produces inside, on or outside its production possibility curve. Syllabus 1.4.2.

The big idea

Where a production point lies compared with the PPC tells you how well an economy is using its resources. A point on the PPC is efficient (full employment of resources). A point inside (under) the PPC is inefficient: resources are wasted. A point outside (beyond) the PPC is unattainable with current resources and technology.

Three possible positions

PositionMeaningPossible reasons
On the PPCFull and efficient use of all resources: maximum outputFull employment; resources allocated efficiently
Inside (under) the PPCResources are unemployed or inefficiently used: the economy could produce more of one or both goodsUnemployment (recession), under-used land or machinery, poor management, lack of skills or mismatch, natural disasters, war
Outside (beyond) the PPCUnattainable with current resources and technologyPossible only if the PPC shifts outwards (economic growth)

What happens when an economy is inside the PPC?

  • It can move up to the curve by using unemployed resources: it can produce more of one or both goods at no opportunity cost.
  • This is what happens when an economy recovers from a recession.
  • Moves are caused by changes in the use of resources, not in the quantity or quality of resources.

An economy can be on the PPC but still produce a combination people do not want. The PPC shows what is possible, not what is best.

Examples are described in general terms so they stay accurate. Check current details before quoting figures.

The 2008 financial crisis

Output fell and unemployment rose in many countries, leaving them well inside their PPC.

Exam use: A recession.

COVID-19 lockdowns

Many economies produced far below capacity in 2020 as factories, shops and services closed.

Exam use: Inside the PPC.

Recovery after a recession

As unemployment falls and firms hire again, output rises back towards capacity.

Exam use: Moving towards the PPC.

War and disasters

Destroyed factories and farmland reduce capacity and push the PPC inwards.

Exam use: A fall in capacity.

Q1 Explain 4 marks Explain what is meant by a point inside a PPC.
ReminderUnemployed or inefficiently used resources; could produce more.
A point inside the PPC means resources are not fully or efficiently used, for example there is unemployment or machinery lies idle. The economy could produce more of one or both goods by using these resources.
Q2 Explain 4 marks Explain why a point outside a PPC is unattainable.
ReminderBeyond what resources and technology allow.
A PPC shows the maximum output possible with the economy's existing resources and technology. A point outside it needs more resources or better technology than the economy has, so it cannot be reached until economic growth shifts the PPC outwards.
Q3 Explain 4 marks Explain why a recession can be shown as a point inside the PPC.
ReminderUnemployment; falling output; resources idle.
In a recession output falls and unemployment rises, so labour and capital are not fully used. The economy is producing less than its maximum, which is shown by a point inside the PPC.
Q4 Analyse 6 marks Analyse why an economy may be producing at a point inside its PPC and how it could move to the curve. (Oct/Nov 2023, P22 style)
ReminderCauses: unemployment, inefficiency, mismatch; policies: demand, training, efficiency.
Knowledge (K)Application (A)Analysis (N)Evaluation (E)
An economy could be inside its PPC because of unemployment during a recession, when demand is low. Skills mismatches and poor management also leave resources under-used. Government spending, lower interest rates or lower taxes could raise demand so unemployed resources are used. Training and better organisation could improve how well resources are matched to jobs. The size of the gain depends on how much spare capacity there is.

Top tips

  • On = efficient; inside = wasteful; outside = unattainable. Learn these three.
  • Moving from inside to the curve has no opportunity cost. Idle resources are used.
  • Outside the curve needs growth. A shift of the PPC.
  • Recession = inside the PPC. Recovery = move towards it.
  • Mark and label points A, B, C. Easier to refer to in your answer.
