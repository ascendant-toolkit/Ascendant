AO1 Knowledge and Understanding: Securing the Recall Marks

Definitions, formulas and terminology: what AO1 tests, which command words signal it and how to earn every mark. Assessment Objective 1.

The big idea

AO1 is knowledge and understanding: recalling definitions, formulas, concepts and theories and using economic terminology correctly. It is 43% of the whole IGCSE, 50% of Paper 1 and 40% of Paper 2, so it is the largest single source of marks you can secure by learning properly. AO1 questions use command words such as Define, Identify, State, Give, Calculate and Describe.

Habits that secure AO1 marks

  • Learn definitions word for word from the syllabus terms: the exact key words earn the marks.
  • Learn the formulas (PED, PES, total revenue, ATC, inflation, unemployment rate) and always show working.
  • Use economic terminology in every answer: say "opportunity cost", "quantity demanded", "price elastic", not everyday words.
  • Match your answer length to the marks: 1 mark per point, no extras.
  • In multiple-choice, cross out wrong options; check units and signs in calculations.

One mark is roughly 1.5 minutes in Paper 2 (80 marks in 120 minutes). A 2-mark define should take about 3 minutes, including reading the question.

Exam-style questions

Open each question for the reminders, a model answer and common mistakes. Answers are colour-coded to the mark scheme. Past-paper questions show the session.

Knowledge (K)Application (A)Analysis (N)Evaluation (E)
Q1 Define 2 marks Define opportunity cost.
ReminderNext best alternative given up.
Opportunity cost is the next best alternative given up when a choice is made.
Q2 Define 2 marks Define price elasticity of demand.
ReminderResponsiveness of quantity demanded to price.
Price elasticity of demand is a measure of the responsiveness of quantity demanded to a change in price.
Q3 Identify 2 marks Identify two functions of money.
ReminderMedium of exchange, store of value.
Medium of exchange. Store of value (or measure of value or deferred payment).
Q4 Calculate 3 marks The price of a good rises from $10 to $12 and quantity demanded falls from 100 to 70. Calculate price elasticity of demand.
Reminder%ΔQd ÷ %ΔP.
%ΔP = 2 ÷ 10 × 100 = 20%; %ΔQd = −30 ÷ 100 × 100 = −30%. PED = −30 ÷ 20 = −1.5.
Q5 Calculate 2 marks A firm sells 200 units at $5 each and has total costs of $700. Calculate its profit.
ReminderTR − TC.
TR = 5 × 200 = $1,000. Profit = 1,000 − 700 = $300.

Top tips

  • Learn the exact definitions. Key terms earn the marks.
  • Show formula, working and unit. Even for 1 mark.
  • Match length to marks. 1.5 minutes per mark.
  • Use terminology. Not everyday words.
  • In Paper 1, eliminate wrong options. Then check the calculation.
