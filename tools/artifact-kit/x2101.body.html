Definition of the Mixed Economic System

What a mixed economy is and how the public and private sectors share the allocation of resources. Syllabus 2.10.1.

The big idea

A mixed economic system combines the private sector (the market, with firms and consumers) and the public sector (the government). Resources are allocated partly by the price mechanism and partly by government decisions. Almost every real economy is mixed; they differ in how much of each they use.

What the public and private sectors do

Private sector

  • Most goods and services: food, cars, clothes, phones
  • Driven by profit and competition
  • Owned by individuals and shareholders

Public sector

  • Public goods (defence, street lighting)
  • Merit goods (education, healthcare)
  • Regulation, taxation and welfare benefits
  • Owned and run by the state

The balance varies: some mixed economies are more market-based (the US), others rely more on the state (the Nordic countries). The public sector often exists to correct market failure.

Sweden

Q1 Define 2 marks Define a mixed economic system.
ReminderBoth market and government.
A mixed economic system is one in which resources are allocated partly by the market (private sector) and partly by the government (public sector).
Q2 Explain 4 marks Explain the roles of the public sector and private sector in a mixed economy.
ReminderPrivate: profit goods; public: public and merit goods, regulation.
The private sector provides most goods and services, driven by profit and competition. The public sector provides public goods and merit goods such as defence and education, and regulates and taxes to correct market failure.
Q3 Explain 4 marks Explain why most countries have a mixed economic system.
ReminderMarkets fail; planned economies inefficient.
A pure market economy ignores public goods, external costs and inequality, so governments need to step in. A fully planned economy is often inefficient and lacks choice, so most countries combine the strengths of both.

Top tips

  • Mixed = market + government. Say both.
  • Public vs private examples. Defence vs smartphones.
  • Link to market failure. The reason for government.
  • Countries differ in the balance. Use examples.
