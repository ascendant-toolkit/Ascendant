Money

Forms, functions and characteristics of money. Syllabus 3.1.1.

The big idea

Money is anything generally accepted as a means of payment for goods and services and for settling debts. It exists because barter (swapping goods directly) needs a double coincidence of wants, which is rare. Money solves this and makes trade, specialisation and saving possible.

Forms of money

FormDescription
Commodity moneyGoods with their own value, such as gold, silver, salt, shells
Coins and notes (cash)Legal tender issued by the central bank; accepted by law
Bank depositsMoney held in bank accounts, used with debit cards, transfers and cheques
Electronic and digital moneyCard payments, mobile payment apps, online transfers, and (not yet universal) cryptocurrencies

Functions of money

FunctionMeaningExample
Medium of exchangeAccepted in payment for goods and services, avoiding barterPaying for lunch with a note or card
Measure of value (unit of account)Prices are stated in money, so values can be comparedA phone costs $300 and a laptop $900
Store of valueMoney can be saved and used later, keeping its purchasing power (if inflation is low)Savings in a bank account
Method of deferred paymentAllows debts and credit to be settled in futureLoans, mortgages, hire purchase

Characteristics of good money

  • Acceptable: widely trusted and accepted.
  • Durable: lasts without wearing out.
  • Portable: easy to carry.
  • Divisible: can be split into small amounts for small purchases.
  • Scarce (limited in supply): so it keeps its value.
  • Uniform: one unit is the same as another.
  • Stable in value: holds its purchasing power.

Q1 Define 2 marks Define money.
ReminderGenerally accepted means of payment.
Money is anything generally accepted as a means of payment for goods and services and to settle debts.
Q2 Identify 2 marks Identify two functions of money.
ReminderMedium of exchange; measure of value; store of value; deferred payment.
Medium of exchange. Store of value (or measure of value).
Q3 Explain 4 marks Explain two characteristics of good money.
ReminderDurable, portable, divisible, acceptable, scarce.
Money should be portable. People can carry it easily to pay for goods. Money should be divisible. It can be split into small amounts for low-priced goods.
Q4 Explain 4 marks Explain why money is better than barter.
ReminderDouble coincidence; measure of value; store.
Barter needs a double coincidence of wants, which is rare. Money is accepted by everyone, so trade is easy. It also gives a measure of value and a way to save.

Top tips

  • Four functions. Exchange, value, store, deferred payment.
  • Link each function to an example. A card payment, a price tag, savings.
  • Characteristics: acceptable, durable, portable, divisible. Scarce and stable too.
  • Barter's problem: double coincidence. Say it.
