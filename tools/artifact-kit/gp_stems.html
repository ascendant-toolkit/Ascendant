Failure-led questions: the pattern in the papers

Most intervention essays in 2020 to 2025 start from a named failure, usually a negative externality, and ask how far one or more policies deal with it. Mark scheme points below are in our own words. Where the table says "from the wording", we have not summarised the mark scheme.

The habit this rewardsIn every one of these, the top marks need (1) the failure named and shown on a labelled diagram, (2) a policy matched to it with the diagram changed to show the effect, and (3) evaluation of that policy: measuring the right level, time lags, cost, elasticity and side effects. A policy that does not fit the failure scores little however well it is described.
