Definition of Price Elasticity of Demand

What PED measures and the difference between elastic and inelastic demand. Syllabus 2.6.1.

The big idea

Price elasticity of demand (PED) measures how responsive the quantity demanded of a good is to a change in its price. If a small price change causes a large change in quantity demanded, demand is elastic. If a large price change causes only a small change in quantity demanded, demand is inelastic.

Why it matters

  • Demand curves slope downwards, but the steepness differs from good to good.
  • PED tells firms what happens to sales and revenue when they change the price, and tells governments what happens when they tax a good.

Elastic and inelastic demand

Elastic demandInelastic demand
ResponseQuantity demanded changes by a bigger percentage than the priceQuantity demanded changes by a smaller percentage than the price
CurveFlatterSteeper
ExamplesBranded chocolate bars, holidays, restaurant meals (many substitutes)Petrol, salt, medicine, cigarettes (few substitutes or necessities)

The word elastic means stretchy: if the price changes, quantity demanded stretches a lot. Inelastic means it hardly moves.

The idea in a sentence

PED = % change in quantity demanded ÷ % change in price

Examples are described in general terms so they stay accurate. Check current details before quoting figures.

Petrol

Drivers keep buying even when prices jump, because there are few short-run substitutes.

Exam use: Inelastic demand.

Streaming subscriptions

Many rivals exist, so a price rise often leads subscribers to cancel.

Exam use: Elastic demand.

Cigarettes

Smokers cut back little when prices rise because of addiction.

Exam use: Inelastic demand.

Foreign holidays

Households quickly switch to cheaper destinations or stay at home when prices rise.

Exam use: Elastic demand.

Top tips

  • PED = responsiveness of Qd to price. Say 'quantity demanded'.
  • Elastic = flat; inelastic = steep. Drawing tip.
  • Substitutes and necessity decide it. Name them.
  • Compare % changes. Bigger or smaller.
