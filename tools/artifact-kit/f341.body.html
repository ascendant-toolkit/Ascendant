Different Types of Firms

Primary, secondary and tertiary firms, private and public sector firms, and small and large firms. Syllabus 3.4.1.

The big idea

Firms can be classified by the sector of the economy they work in (primary, secondary, tertiary), by ownership (private or public sector) and by size (small or large). Each type has its own strengths and weaknesses.

By sector

SectorWhat it doesExamples
PrimaryExtracts or grows natural resourcesFarming, fishing, mining, oil extraction, forestry
SecondaryManufactures and builds, turning raw materials into productsCar makers, bakeries, steel mills, construction
TertiaryProvides servicesBanks, hospitals, shops, teachers, tourism, transport

As countries develop, the economy typically shifts from primary to secondary and then to tertiary activity.

By ownership

SectorOwned byMain aimExamples
Private sectorIndividuals or shareholdersProfitSole traders, partnerships, companies
Public sectorThe governmentProviding a service; social welfare; sometimes profitState schools, public hospitals, state railways

Small and large firms

Small firms

Advantages: personal service, flexible, quick decisions, close to customers, easy to start, owner motivated.
Disadvantages: limited finance, no economies of scale, higher unit costs, risk of failure, limited bargaining power.

Large firms

Advantages: economies of scale (lower unit costs), easier to raise finance, market power, can afford R&D and advertising, diversify risk.
Disadvantages: diseconomies of scale, slower decisions, less personal, may be remote from customers, may abuse market power.

Q1 Identify 3 marks Identify the primary, secondary and tertiary sector firm in this list: a bank, a coal mine, a car factory.
ReminderCoal mine; car factory; bank.
Primary: coal mine. Secondary: car factory. Tertiary: bank.
Q2 Explain 4 marks Explain two advantages of small firms.
ReminderPersonal service, flexibility.
Small firms can offer personal service. They know their customers, which builds loyalty. They can make decisions quickly. They can respond to changes in demand faster than large firms.
Q3 Explain 4 marks Explain two advantages of large firms.
ReminderEconomies of scale; finance.
Large firms benefit from economies of scale, so cost per unit is lower. They find it easier to raise finance, so they can invest and expand.
Q4 Explain 4 marks Explain the difference between private sector and public sector firms.
ReminderOwnership; aims.
Private sector firms are owned by individuals or shareholders and aim mainly to make a profit. Public sector firms are owned by the government and aim to provide a service or promote social welfare.

Top tips

  • Primary: extract; secondary: make; tertiary: serve. Use examples.
  • Private: profit; public: service. Ownership and aims.
  • Small: personal, flexible; large: scale. Both sides.
  • As economies develop, tertiary grows. Useful evaluation.
