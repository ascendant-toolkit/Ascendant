Definitions of Costs of Production

Total, fixed, variable, average fixed, average variable and average total cost. Syllabus 3.6.1.

The big idea

Firms face costs of production. Fixed costs do not change with output; variable costs do. Adding them gives total cost. Dividing each by output gives the average (per unit) costs. Know all six definitions exactly.

The six cost measures

TermDefinitionFormulaExample
Fixed cost (FC)Costs that do not change with output in the short run—Rent, insurance, salaries, loan interest
Variable cost (VC)Costs that change directly with output—Raw materials, wages of production workers, energy used
Total cost (TC)All the costs of producing a given outputTC = FC + VC$100 + $200 = $300
Average fixed cost (AFC)Fixed cost per unit of outputAFC = FC ÷ output$100 ÷ 50 = $2
Average variable cost (AVC)Variable cost per unit of outputAVC = VC ÷ output$200 ÷ 50 = $4
Average total cost (ATC)Total cost per unit of outputATC = TC ÷ output = AFC + AVC$300 ÷ 50 = $6
  • Fixed costs have to be paid even when output is zero; they are only fixed in the short run.
  • AFC always falls as output rises (fixed cost is spread over more units).
  • AVC usually falls and then rises (diminishing returns).
  • ATC is the sum of the two, so it is U-shaped.

Costs can be paid in cash (explicit) or be the value of owner-supplied resources. Always match a cost to its type with an example from the question.

Knowledge (K)Application (A)Analysis (N)Evaluation (E)
Q1 Define 2 marks Define fixed costs.
ReminderDo not change with output.
Fixed costs are costs that do not change when output changes in the short run.
Q2 Define 2 marks Define average total cost.
ReminderTotal cost per unit.
Average total cost is total cost divided by the number of units produced.
Q3 Identify 2 marks Identify one fixed cost and one variable cost for a bakery.
ReminderRent; flour.
Fixed cost: rent of the bakery. Variable cost: flour.
Q4 Explain 4 marks Explain the difference between fixed and variable costs.
ReminderFixed constant; variable change with output.
Fixed costs do not change with output, for example rent has to be paid even if no goods are made. Variable costs change with output, for example more raw materials are needed when output rises.

Top tips

  • Six definitions. FC, VC, TC, AFC, AVC, ATC.
  • Give a business example. Rent vs flour.
  • TC = FC + VC; ATC = TC ÷ Q. Memorise.
  • Fixed in the short run only. All costs vary in the long run.
