Competitive Markets

Characteristics, advantages and disadvantages of competitive markets and the effect of many firms on price, quality, choice and profit. Syllabus 3.7.1.

The big idea

A competitive market has many firms selling similar products to many buyers, with no single firm able to control the price and easy entry for new firms. Competition keeps prices low, quality high and choice wide, and keeps profits at a normal level. (Diagrams and perfect or imperfect competition theory are not required.)

Characteristics of competitive markets

  • A large number of firms and buyers.
  • Easy entry and exit: low barriers to entry for new firms.
  • Similar or identical products, or close substitutes.
  • Good information: buyers and sellers know prices.
  • Firms are price takers or have little power over price.

Effect of having many firms

VariableEffect of many firms competing
PriceLower: firms undercut each other to win customers
QualityHigher: firms improve quality to attract buyers
ChoiceWider: many products and variants
ProfitLower: competition limits profit; new firms enter if profits are high
Efficiency and innovationHigher: firms cut costs and innovate to survive

Advantages

  • Lower prices for consumers
  • Better quality and choice
  • Efficient use of resources
  • Firms respond to consumer wants
  • Innovation to stay ahead

Disadvantages

  • Low profit may leave little for research
  • Small firms may lack economies of scale
  • Firms may cut quality or wages to cut costs
  • Many firms may fail: job losses
  • Wasteful duplication or excessive advertising

Q1 Identify 2 marks Identify two characteristics of a competitive market.
ReminderMany firms, easy entry, similar products.
A large number of firms. Easy entry for new firms (or little control over price).
Q2 Explain 4 marks Explain the effect of having many firms in a market on price and quality.
ReminderLower prices; higher quality.
With many firms competing, each tries to win customers by cutting prices. Prices fall. Firms also improve quality to stand out, so consumers get better products.
Q3 Explain 4 marks Explain the effect of many firms on profit.
ReminderCompetition limits profit; entry.
If profits are high, new firms enter the market easily. Supply rises and price falls, so profit per firm falls to a normal level.
Q4 Discuss 8 marks Discuss whether competitive markets are good for consumers.
ReminderPrice, quality, choice vs quality cuts and little research. Judgement.
Knowledge (K)Application (A)Analysis (N)Evaluation (E)
In competitive markets many firms compete, so prices tend to be low. Quality and choice are higher as firms try to win customers. Competition also encourages efficiency and innovation. However, low profits may leave little money for research, and some firms may cut quality or wages to survive. Overall, competitive markets are usually good for consumers, particularly where information is good and regulation prevents harmful cost cutting.

Top tips

  • Many firms, easy entry. Say both.
  • Effects: price, quality, choice, profit. The syllabus list.
  • Use an example market. Bakeries, street food, taxis.
  • Evaluate. Low profit, quality cutting.
