Use of Operators

Arithmetic, comparison and logical operators, including DIV, MOD and AND/OR/NOT.

The big idea

Operators work on values. Arithmetic operators calculate, comparison operators give TRUE or FALSE, and logical operators (AND, OR, NOT) combine those results.

8.1.4 Use of operators

An operator is a symbol or word that does something to values. There are three groups: arithmetic (calculate), comparison (compare, giving TRUE or FALSE) and logical (combine TRUE/FALSE results). You have already used many of them. This page puts them together.

DIV and MOD together17 divided by 5 is 3 remainder 2. DIV gives the 3 and MOD gives the 2. (Some books write them as functions, DIV(17, 5) and MOD(17, 5).) Useful jobs: X MOD 2 = 0 tests for an even number. X MOD 10 gives the last digit. Minutes DIV 60 and Minutes MOD 60 turn minutes into hours and minutes.

Order of operations

The computer works out brackets first, then ^, then * and / (left to right), then + and -. Use brackets to force the order you want.

4 · Multiple choice

Descriptions are kept general. Check current details before quoting specifics.

A clock

Converting 135 minutes into hours and minutes uses DIV and MOD. A 24-hour clock wraps around using MOD 24.

Exam use: DIV gives whole units, MOD gives what is left over.

A funfair ride

A rider must be tall enough and old enough. A child may ride if they are with an adult or are tall enough.

Exam use: AND needs both conditions TRUE. OR needs only one.

Odd and even

A game gives bonus points on even-numbered levels. The program tests Level MOD 2 = 0.

Exam use: MOD 2 = 0 means even; MOD 2 = 1 means odd.

Bank interest

Interest is worked out with a power: the balance multiplied by 1.05 for each year, which is 1.05 ^ Years.

Exam use: ^ is used for powers; use brackets to control the order.

Q1 State 2 marks State what the operators DIV and MOD do.
ReminderGive an example for each, using 17 and 5.
DIV: gives the whole number part of a division (for example 17 DIV 5 = 3) MOD: gives the remainder of a division (for example 17 MOD 5 = 2).
Common mistakeMixing them up, or saying DIV is the same as /. 17 / 5 is 3.4.

Top tips

  • DIV is the whole number, MOD is the remainder. 17 DIV 5 = 3 and 17 MOD 5 = 2.
  • X MOD 2 = 0 means X is even.
  • Order: brackets, ^, * and /, then + and -. Add brackets when unsure.
  • Each side of AND or OR is a full condition. Age >= 13 AND Age <= 19.
  • AND needs both TRUE. OR needs at least one TRUE. NOT reverses.
  • In pseudocode ← stores and = compares. In Python = stores and == compares.
