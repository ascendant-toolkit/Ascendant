Artificial Intelligence and its Impact

Coursebook: Chapter 7 · Ethics and ownership · section 7.1 Ethics and ownership

How facial recognition, speech recognition, optical character recognition and game-playing AI work, and the social, economic and ethical impacts of AI, with animations, a match-threshold lab and the real Paper 1 questions from 2021 to 2026.

This page in five sentences

Artificial intelligence (AI) is the ability of a computer system to perform tasks that normally need human intelligence, such as recognising faces, understanding speech and playing games, by using data, patterns and training. AI systems are trained on large amounts of data and learn to make a prediction, such as the probability that an image matches a face. Typical applications are image and facial recognition, speech recognition and natural language processing, optical character recognition, self-driving cars and game playing. AI has social, economic and ethical impacts, both positive and negative; exam answers describe one clearly and link it to the situation. The explaining questions ask how a given system uses AI, with a chain such as camera, pixels, features, comparison with a database and a probability of a match.

1 · How AI is used in common systems

SystemHow it uses AI
Facial recognition (phone unlock, shop entry, CCTV)The camera captures an image; image recognition identifies that it is a face by analysing the pixels for patterns; the distances between facial features are measured and compared with those stored in a database; the system uses the probability of a match. It is trained using a large number of images
CCTV trackingImage recognition monitors every image for shapes or features that match a person; recording starts when a person is identified; the direction of movement is used to decide where to move the camera or which camera to start
Reading registration plates (optical character recognition)The camera image is stored as pixels; the area of the plate is found; the pixels are matched to expected characters, letters and numbers
Voice assistants and robots (speech and natural language)Speech recognition identifies the words or key phrases spoken and matches them to a database; natural language processing combines them into the most likely sentence or command and the programmed action is performed
Reading and translating textOptical character recognition finds characters in the photograph; natural language processing combines them into words and compares them with a database in the chosen language; text-to-speech generates audio
Game playingThe rules and past moves are stored; the AI is trained by playing many times; it looks ahead at possible moves and chooses the move most likely to succeed; it learns from past mistakes by storing the result of each choice and changing future choices
Self-driving carsSensors and cameras detect the car's position on the road and in traffic; it follows a route, avoids collisions and parks itself

2 · Impacts of AI

ImpactPositiveNegative
SocialHelps visually impaired people choose products; helps people who cannot understand a language; helps with learning or reading difficulties; individuals may feel safer and crime may be reduced; early help for students who strugglePrivacy concerns about constant surveillance and data passed to third parties; mistakes such as incorrect recognition of faces (people denied access); misinformation if an AI answer is wrong; students may miss out on developing reasoning skills; digital divide; stress and loneliness
EconomicLower costs (for example faster fault diagnosis in a garage so technicians complete more jobs); higher profits; lower prices passed to customersThe AI program may be expensive to buy, maintain and update, which reduces profit margins; jobs may be lost
EthicalMore personalised learning; improved safetySurveillance and consent; protection of intellectual property; bias and incorrect results

Common ways to lose marks

  • Writing that AI "thinks like a human". Describe the actual steps: image recognition, comparison with a database, probability of a match.
  • Describing only what the system does (recognises a face) and not how AI does it.
  • Giving an impact with no explanation. "Privacy concerns" needs "because people are under constant surveillance".
  • Giving a positive impact when the question asks for an ethical impact only if it concerns ethics (privacy, consent, surveillance, bias).
  • Confusing AI with simple programming. AI is trained on data and works with probabilities.

Examples are explained in general terms. Check current details before quoting them in an exam answer.

Unlocking your phone with your face

The phone measures the distances between your facial features and compares them with the stored values: image recognition and a probability of a match.

Exam use: facial recognition.

Voice assistants

You speak a request; the assistant identifies the words, matches them to commands and replies: speech recognition and natural language processing.

Exam use: speech recognition.

Number plate cameras in car parks

The camera finds the plate and optical character recognition turns the pixels into the registration number.

Exam use: OCR.

A chess program

It looks ahead at possible moves and picks the one most likely to win, improving from experience.

Exam use: game-playing AI.

A translation app that reads signs

The camera reads the text, the app translates it and says it aloud: it helps people who cannot read the language.

Exam use: OCR, language processing, text-to-speech.

M/J 2021 · 9618/12 · Q2(b) Describe 3 marks The team are developing a computer game where the user plays a board game (such as chess) against the computer. Describe how the computer would use Artificial Intelligence (AI) to play the board game.
ReminderRules and moves stored, trained, look ahead, choose, learn.

Any three: The rules, past moves or decision-making algorithms of the game are stored The AI program is trained by playing many times The AI looks ahead at possible moves and/or analyses the pattern of past choices and chooses the move most likely to be successful The computer could learn from previous mistakes by storing the result of choices and changing its future choices

ReminderName an application and describe it.

One mark for the application and up to two for the description, for example: Police identifying wanted people: uses image recognition to identify features or characteristics in an image Natural language interface: uses speech recognition to identify spoken words and adapts to learn regional accents Self-driving cars: detects its position on the road and in traffic, follows a route, avoids collisions Game playing: models characters so they react according to the player's movements

O/N 2022 · 9618/13 · Q8(b) Describe 2 marks A commercial program for a vehicle repair garage includes an AI module that can diagnose faults and suggest repairs. Describe one economic impact the AI module may have on the garage.
ReminderCosts and profits, with a reason.

One impact and one expansion: Reduces costs to the garage because less time is taken for diagnosis Increases profits because technicians spend more time repairing, completing more jobs in a day Decreases costs passed to customers, so the garage may gain customers Profit margins can be reduced because the program may be expensive to buy, maintain or update

M/J 2025 · 9618/12 · Q3(a)(b) Explain / State 4 + 2 marks A computer program uses a digital camera to read the words on an item, translates them to a chosen language and outputs the words as audio. ReminderOCR, natural language, text-to-speech. Visual impairment, language, reading difficulty.

O/N 2025 · 9618/11 · Q8(c) Describe 4 marks Describe two social impacts of students using Artificial Intelligence to complete their homework.
ReminderReasoning skills, communication, digital divide, wrong answers; support, self-esteem.

O/N 2025 · 9618/13 · Q7(a) Describe 2 marks A digital video camera records students in a classroom. AI is used to analyse the video on the server to identify when students are interacting with the lesson and the teacher. Describe one ethical impact of this use of AI in the classroom.
ReminderPrivacy, surveillance, mental health, or improved learning.

One impact and its expansion: The learning experience could be improved by identifying students who are struggling, with more personalised learning or early intervention There could be privacy concerns because students or teachers may be uncomfortable with constant surveillance, or may not want their data passed to third parties There could be mental health concerns due to constant monitoring and pressure, contributing to student stress and anxiety

It learns the patterns that identify what it is looking for more data makes the probability of a correct match higher

ReminderCosts, jobs.

Explain 3 marks Explain how speech recognition could be used by a voice assistant to set an alarm.

The microphone records the speech Speech recognition identifies the key words and matches them to a database The most likely command is generated and the alarm is set

Top tips

  • Give the chain. Capture, analyse, compare, decide.
  • Name the technique. Image recognition, speech recognition, OCR, NLP.
  • Probability of a match. AI works with likelihood, not certainty.
  • Impact plus reason. Social: privacy because of surveillance.
  • Trained on large data. Say it for any AI question.
  • Use the scenario's words. The shop, the robot, the airport.
